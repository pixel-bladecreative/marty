Before You Start
Build all your assets first — the product, the character, the locations, the props. They recur in every scene, and locking them up front is what keeps everything consistent once the camera starts moving.
Three tools do the building. 
GPT Image 2
 handles product sheets, edits and schematic maps. 
Soul Cinema and Cinematic Locations
 handle the photoreal stills — the character, the locations. The scenes themselves are generated in 
Seedance 2.0
.
You don't write the Seedance prompts by hand. A Claude Skill does it for you — generating the shotlist and per-scene prompts in the exact format Seedance expects. Just download the skill, describe your scene, and let it handle the formatting.

Customize → Skills
 and upload it. Open a fresh chat and attach the script plus every asset you locked in Stage 1 — the more Claude can actually see, the better the prompts come back.

Last, in Higgsfield add each asset under Elements and name it exactly the same. Because the names match, every prompt the skill writes auto-attaches the right images when you generate.

Stage 3 — The Scenes
Scene 1 — 1a · Kitchen 
What it does:
 Animates the open: the hero walks into the kitchen, clocks the neighbour's lawnmower through the window, slips the headphones on, and the roar drops to a hum.
Style header:
 The top of the prompt locks the look — lens, lighting, handheld feel and slow-mo — and every other scene reuses it, so the whole ad feels like one film. It also keeps the sound to real-world noise only, no music — you add the music later in the edit.
Style: 8K IMAX commercial, 16:9 widescreen. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine. Lighting: Natural light only — soft, even morning daylight, gentle atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and garden doors only. No contre-jour, no rim backlight, no forced shadow-side framing. No artificial lightning. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent. Camera: Physical cine lens. 180° shutter motion blur. Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light. Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, living eyes with catch-lights, chest rise from breathing. Characters never standing, always reacting. Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props. Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one. Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift. Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter. Audio: Diegetic dialogue and environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.
Characters: HERO (@hero) — lean man, late 20s, reddish-brown curly hair, fair freckled skin, small gold hoop earrings, dusty-rose short-sleeve shirt open over a white tee, light-wash baggy jeans, black-and-white low-top Converse. Cream/ivory over-ear ANC headphones (@headphones — tan camel-leather cushions, thin orange accent ring) rest on the counter to the right of the gas range; a stovetop moka pot, an empty espresso cup, the cream running sneakers (@sneakers) and the open dark-green canvas backpack (@bag) sit on the same counter.
Scene: A sage-green country kitchen (@kitchen), early morning. A stainless gas range sits under a window on the left wall, with a stretch of counter to the right of it (moka pot, empty cup, headphones, sneakers and backpack on it); a white marble island fills the centre; French glass garden doors stand stage-right (his exit). Through the window above the range a NEIGHBOUR is mowing the lawn outside. Soft, even morning daylight throughout. A lawnmower drone is already droning outside from the first frame.
CUT 1 — Behind Hero into profile, 35mm, slow follow as he steps to the range: Hero crosses empty-handed to the gas range on the left wall; the camera starts behind him — his back and curly head — then eases round to his side profile. Through the window above the range the NEIGHBOUR pushes a roaring lawnmower across the lawn, grass spraying — the neighbour established here, once, this single time. Hero watches it for a beat, then a small amused exhale — a quiet "heh" — tugs one corner of his mouth.
CUT 2 — Medium close at the counter, 50mm, push-in to chest: Hero picks up the cream headphones (@headphones) from the counter and lifts them over his ears in one smooth motion; the cushioned cups seal and the roar drops into a low muffled hum — shoulders dropping a full inch, eyes half-closing for a beat, a calm exhale, his head already starting to bob. CUT 3 — Jump-cut coffee montage, 50mm, three hard in-camera jump-cuts, Hero grooving through every stage: STAGE 1 — he sets the stovetop moka pot onto the gas range; a small, low amber flame catches under it, kept minimal, barely licking the base. JUMP CUT. STAGE 2 — closer in: the moka pot now gurgling on the flame, a curl of steam rising, Hero's fingers drumming the counter to the beat. JUMP CUT. STAGE 3 — he pours the fresh coffee into the espresso cup, lifts it and takes an unhurried sip, a soft satisfied smile spreading, head still nodding to the beat.
