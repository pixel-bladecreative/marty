## Production look rules (client-mandated)

- LOOK: crisp deep focus and clean air in every shot. No atmospheric haze,
  no mist, no dreamy soft focus. Shallow depth of field only where a shot
  explicitly calls for it (macros, the skip close-up).
- HEIGHT: every prompt that puts Marty next to a human restates the anchor:
  Marty is 6 ft; beside the 5'7" coworker his eye line sits slightly above
  hers, top of his head about 5 inches over. Never shorter than her.
