## Keep, no changes

  laugh beat, the press and its lighting, their interaction.

## Fix: environment (the big one)
- The office reads as a backdrop the characters perform OVER, not a 3D space
  they are IN. Haze/mist persisted into the video (baked into the office
  plates; prompt text could not fully override hazy reference images).
- The office materializes behind the door instead of opening into real space.
- Decision: REDO THE OFFICE ENVIRONMENT ENTIRELY. New plates: crisp,
  commercially lit, bright, real 3D depth, zero haze. Characters must be lit
  the same as the room.
- Add 1-2 background extras doing real work. Not more.

## Fix: the bully (rework altogether)
- He pops into existence: no entrance, lighting mismatch, never looks at
  Marty, Marty never looks at him, pops back out.
- Restage: a real entrance walk, eye contact both ways, same light as the
  room, then the slap.

## Fix: label continuity (story-critical)
- The DORK label she peels must be THE SAME label, in THE SAME chest
  position, that the bully slapped on.
- The LEGEND label must be one consistent object from drawer to chest, and
  read as a real Sticker Mountain product (embossed foil).
- Enforce in prompts: identical label refs in every scene, explicit position
  language, continuity clauses.

## Fix: Marty's entry gait
- Keep the spring, make the movement a bit more human as he enters.
