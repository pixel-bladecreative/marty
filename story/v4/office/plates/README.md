# Office plates (direction E3)

One room, four camera positions. P1 is the master still. P2 to P4 are frames
from `../tour/tour-A.mp4` (Seedance 2.5, 15 s, 720p, started on P1) re-rendered
sharp at 2k with P1 as a second reference, so geometry comes from the tour and
materials from the master.

| Plate | Camera looks at | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| P1-master | Desks left, meeting pod right, windows behind | Wide establishing, the friend's desk, walk-off |
| P2-pod-printer | Open floor, pod glass, edge of printer | Mid shots on the open floor, the slap, the peel |
| P3-printer-rack | Printer, sample board, label-roll rack, far desks | Bully's loitering spot, close shots with the rack behind |
| P4-door-wall | Desk rows, grey door in the far wall | Marty's entry through the door, reverse angles |

## Floor plan

- Entry: the grey door in P4.
- Open floor: about 10 ft of clear carpet between the desk row and the pod. All
  standing action happens here.
- Bully's spot: at the printer, beside the mustard pod end.
- Friend's desk: the near desk in P1, by the plant.
- Keep action a few feet off the pod glass to avoid reflection work.

## Scale rulers

Ceiling 9 ft with a 2 ft tile grid. Doors 6 ft 8 in. Desks 29 in. Printer top
about 45 in. Marty is 6 ft: his head clears the door frame by about 8 in, and
sits about 3 ft under the ceiling.
