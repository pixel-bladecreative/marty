Style: high-end cinema commercial, 16:9. Live-action photorealistic world and people, with one animated character living in it. Lighting: natural light only, clear mid-morning sun, neutral-white daylight with slight warmth, never orange or golden, no lens flare; directional, with crisp soft-edged shadows and real falloff into shade; indoors the sun comes through the windows as bars of light across the carpet and the ceiling lights are off. No added rim light, no glow. Color: 60:30:10, clean neutrals / charcoal / mustard accent. Camera: physical cine lens, handheld-steady with gentle drift, always moving, 180 degree shutter motion blur, shallow depth of field on close shots. Acting: micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-lines, living eyes with catch-lights, breathing. Characters never stand posed, they are always mid-action. Physics: real weight, heel-to-toe footfalls, correct contact shadows, nobody passes through furniture or objects. Composition: rule of thirds, foreground objects framing the shot, every person moving from frame one. Continuity: every character is the identical individual in every cut, matching their reference sheet exactly in face, proportions, colours and clothing; props and locations identical across every cut. 24fps smooth motion. Clear air, no haze. Audio: natural ambience and footsteps only. No music, no dialogue, no subtitles, no on-screen text.

Characters: MARTY (@Image3) is Sticker Mountain's own original brand mascot, a friendly yeti, an original character and not from any film. He must match his reference sheet exactly in every cut and from every angle: 6 feet tall; clean bright white fur that is dense, soft and evenly groomed, never grey, never long or shaggy; two large thick curved ridged teal horns, each as long as his face is tall, the same full size in close-ups, profiles and back views; teal four-fingered hands and teal three-toed feet; big round eyes with teal irises; grey eyebrows; a wide mouth with a dark blue-grey lip line; no nose. Build: athletic V-shape, broad shoulders, deep chest, narrow waist, thick arms, sturdy legs, never skinny, never fat, never hunched into a round ball. He is physically in the scene: the sun falls on his fur as it does on everything around him and he casts a real shadow. He stands with drooping shoulders and the crooked DORK sticker on his chest at the start; his hands are empty. FRIEND (@Image4) must match her reference sheet exactly in every cut: a young woman of 25, fresh-faced, with smooth youthful skin and no lines or wrinkles at all, bright hazel-green eyes, a warm friendly smile, shoulder-length wavy chestnut brown hair with bangs; the same young face in every cut and every light, never older than 25. Olive green jacket open over a white v-neck top, charcoal jeans, white sneakers. She is 5 foot 7 and slim; Marty is 6 feet tall and twice as wide as she is. Whenever they stand together the top of her head comes only to his eye level, his head and horns rise clearly above her, and his shoulders are far broader than hers. The DORK sticker (@Image5) is a paper-thin matte name-tag label, red band on top, the word DORK in black marker, about 4 inches wide, lying flat on fur and bending like paper. Once applied it sits crooked in the exact centre of Marty's chest at breastbone height and stays there. The LEGEND sticker (@Image6) is a paper-thin glossy die-cut label shaped like a mountain crest, teal with the word LEGEND in flat gold foil that glints in the sun, about 4 inches wide, flexible like film, never a badge or plaque. Once applied it sits straight in the exact centre of Marty's chest at breastbone height, the same spot the DORK sticker was, and never moves. 

Scene: a lived-in open-plan office on a sunny morning: rows of white desks with mustard yellow dividers, grey carpet crossed by bars of window sunlight, a glass-fronted meeting pod with a mustard end panel, and beside that panel a white printer and a rack of coloured label rolls. Three or four coworkers work at desks or walk past in the background, soft and out of focus (@Image1 shows the printer side, @Image2 the desks by the windows). Marty's spot for this scene is the open carpet right beside the white printer and the mustard panel, where the Bully stopped him. His paper coffee cup sits on top of the printer.

CUT 1 - Tracking behind the Friend at shoulder height, 35mm, following her walk across the open carpet through the bars of sunlight: she carries the LEGEND sticker in her right hand; ahead of her Marty stands beside the printer with his head down, clearly taller and much broader than her as she reaches him, and he looks up as she arrives.

CUT 2 - Close-up on the centre of Marty's chest with her left hand, 85mm, shallow focus, camera still, the action played slowly and clearly: the peel is one continuous cause-and-effect action led by her fingers: her thumbnail lifts the top corner of the DORK sticker nearest her; she pinches that lifted corner between thumb and forefinger; then she pulls her hand slowly away and downward on a diagonal. The sticker follows her fingers and only her fingers: it is always attached to her pinch at one end and to his fur at the other, bending in a soft curve between them. A single peel line travels diagonally across the sticker at the same speed as her hand; the part she has not reached yet stays stuck flat and still on his fur. The white fur under the peel line tugs outward for an instant and springs back. When her hand has travelled the full width of the sticker the last corner releases with a tiny snap and the label hangs limp from her fingers. The sticker never moves on its own, never slides, and never lifts before her fingers pull it.

CUT 3 - Macro on the centre of his chest, 100mm, shallow focus, slow push-in: with only her right hand, she lays the LEGEND sticker flat onto the exact spot where the DORK sticker was and smooths it down once with her thumb from the centre outward; the paper-thin label settles into the white fur and the gold foil lettering glints in the window sun. Her other hand stays out of frame.

CUT 4 - Two-shot from just behind her shoulder, 50mm, slight low angle looking up at him: her young face in soft profile at the left edge, the top of her head level with his eyes; Marty towers above, his eyes lifting from the sticker in the centre of his chest to her, and the first small, surprised smile starts at one corner of his mouth.
