Style: high-end cinema commercial, 16:9. Live-action photorealistic world and people, with one animated character living in it. Lighting: natural light only, clear mid-morning sun, neutral-white daylight with slight warmth, never orange or golden, no lens flare; directional, with crisp soft-edged shadows and real falloff into shade; indoors the sun comes through the windows as bars of light across the carpet and the ceiling lights are off. No added rim light, no glow. Color: 60:30:10, clean neutrals / charcoal / mustard accent. Camera: physical cine lens, handheld-steady with gentle drift, always moving, 180 degree shutter motion blur, shallow depth of field on close shots. Acting: micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-lines, living eyes with catch-lights, breathing. Characters never stand posed, they are always mid-action. Physics: real weight, heel-to-toe footfalls, correct contact shadows, nobody passes through furniture or objects. Composition: rule of thirds, foreground objects framing the shot, every person moving from frame one. Continuity: every character is the identical individual in every cut, matching their reference sheet exactly in face, proportions, colours and clothing; props and locations identical across every cut. 24fps smooth motion. Clear air, no haze. Audio: natural ambience and footsteps only. No music, no dialogue, no subtitles, no on-screen text.

Characters: MARTY (@Image3) is Sticker Mountain's own original brand mascot, a friendly yeti, an original character and not from any film. He must match his reference sheet exactly in every cut and from every angle: 6 feet tall; clean bright white fur that is dense, soft and evenly groomed, never grey, never long or shaggy; two large thick curved ridged teal horns, each as long as his face is tall, the same full size in close-ups, profiles and back views; teal four-fingered hands and teal three-toed feet; big round eyes with teal irises; grey eyebrows; a wide mouth with a dark blue-grey lip line; no nose. Build: athletic V-shape, broad shoulders, deep chest, narrow waist, thick arms, sturdy legs, never skinny, never fat, never hunched into a round ball. He is physically in the scene: the sun falls on his fur as it does on everything around him and he casts a real shadow. He carries a paper coffee cup with a brown sleeve in his right hand through the whole scene and starts happy. BULLY (@Image4) matches his reference sheet exactly: a lanky 6 foot 1 office worker with a receding hairline and goatee, grey fleece vest over a red checked shirt, beige chinos, lanyard; eye to eye with Marty but far narrower; smug and petty, comic and never threatening. The Bully has no drink and no cup at any point: the only coffee cup in the scene is the one in Marty's right hand. The Bully's left hand stays tucked in his chinos pocket for the whole scene, and his right hand holds only the sticker. The DORK sticker (@Image5) is a paper-thin matte name-tag label, red band on top, the word DORK in black marker, about 4 inches wide, lying flat on fur and bending like paper. Once applied it sits crooked in the exact centre of Marty's chest at breastbone height and stays there. 

Scene: a lived-in open-plan office on a sunny morning: rows of white desks with mustard yellow dividers, grey carpet crossed by bars of window sunlight, a glass-fronted meeting pod with a mustard end panel, and beside that panel a white printer and a rack of coloured label rolls. Three or four coworkers work at desks or walk past in the background, soft and out of focus (@Image1 and @Image2 show the same room from two angles). The Bully stands beside the printer with his shoulder against the mustard panel, left hand in his pocket, peeling the DORK sticker off a small backing sheet with his right.

CUT 1 - Following close behind Marty's shoulder, 35mm, handheld follow easing round toward his profile, his full-size teal horn large in the foreground: he strolls across the open carpet toward the printer through the bars of sunlight; ahead, soft in the background, the Bully waits by the printer with one hand in his pocket and the sticker pinched in the other, looks up and smirks. The Bully holds no cup.

CUT 2 - Medium two-shot beside the printer, 50mm, slight push-in, the rack of coloured rolls soft behind them: the Bully steps into Marty's path and, with his right arm only, reaches straight forward at chest height and presses the DORK sticker onto the centre of Marty's chest, his palm flat against the fur, the sticker deliberately crooked. His hand never rises above shoulder height; it is a press on the chest, not a wave and not a high five. He keeps his palm there for a full beat, gives the sticker two small pats, smirks, and walks away past the camera, left hand still in his pocket, right hand empty. Marty stands still with his coffee, puzzled.

CUT 3 - Insert close-up on Marty's chest, 85mm, shallow focus, slow push-in: the DORK name-tag sticker stuck in the centre of his white chest fur, tilted about 15 degrees off level, one corner slightly lifted, clearly readable, a few fur strands caught under its edge; his free teal hand rises and hovers near it without touching.

CUT 4 - Close-up on Marty's face, 85mm, shallow focus, very slow push-in: he looks down toward his chest and his big grin collapses in stages, the corners of his mouth dropping, his eyebrows tilting up in the middle, his eyes going wide and hurt. He holds there, breathing.
