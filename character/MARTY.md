# Marty — character canon

Marty is Sticker Mountain's mascot: a friendly yeti. This document is the
single source of truth for every generation prompt. If a generated image
disagrees with this document, the image is wrong.

Source references: two images supplied by Spencer (a 2D illustration of Marty
leaping with a red heart balloon, and a photo of a die-cut Marty sticker held
against a Colorado mountain backdrop), plus the site's own mascot art pulled
from stickermountain.com (`reference/yeti-climbing.png`, detail crop at
`reference/marty-climbing-detail.png`) — the cleanest, highest-detail source
and the color authority below. Vector originals from the client would still
sharpen every sheet.

## Identity

- Species: yeti. Friendly, not fearsome.
- Role: Sticker Mountain's mascot and on-screen personality. He exists in the
  real world, alongside real people, at real scale.
- Register: Pixar-grade CG creature in live-action footage. The world around
  him is strictly photoreal; Marty is the only stylized element in frame.

## Physical canon

### Build and silhouette

Revised per client feedback 2026-08-28. Round 1's chest-height round build is
superseded; the v1 sheets stay in the repo for history.

- Bipedal, athletic wild-animal build under the fur. Broad shoulders, deep
  chest, tapered waist, muscular arms, longer legs. A beast's frame with a
  best friend's face: the power is in the body, the warmth is in the face.
- Scale: 1.83 m / 6 ft standing. Eye to eye with adults; the big friendly
  buddy who fills a doorway, not a small sidekick.
- Two horns, one each side, curving up and out from the upper skull, with
  soft segment ridges along their length.
- Four fingers per hand, three toes per foot. Fingertips and toes end in soft
  rounded points, not claws.
- No tail.
- Head large for the frame but less than the v1 third-of-height; long arms,
  fingertips near the knee; shoulders clearly wider than hips.

Rule of thumb: white beast, teal-blue trim, one warm accent (the mouth
interior). Blue appears only at horns, hands, feet, lips and irises. Nothing
else on him is blue.

### Face

The face carries the brand. Lock these:

- Large round eyes, white sclera, teal-blue irises with dark pupils. Thin
  expressive dark eyebrows that do most of the acting. He winks often.
- Wide mouth with a big open grin as the default expression: a friendly row
  of big white teeth top and bottom, dark blue lips, raspberry-red tongue and
  mouth interior. The warm mouth against the cool palette is the face's focal
  point.
- No muzzle to speak of; the face sits flat in the fur. No visible nose in
  the source art. The CG translation may add the subtlest bump only if the
  face reads as incomplete in 3/4 views; never a prominent snout.

### Fur (the CG translation)

- Feature-film groomed fur, Monsters Inc. Sulley grade: dense, directional,
  slightly clumped, with flyaways at silhouette edges.
- Length map: longest on head crown, cheeks, chest and shoulders; shorter on
  muzzle, around eyes, belly; near-shorn into the blue skin of hands and feet.
- Fur responds to the world: wind, inertia, contact flattening where he leans
  or is touched, environment light and color spill picked up in the coat.
- Under real lighting the white coat takes the scene's color temperature.
  Never render him flat white in a warm or blue scene.

## Personality and movement

- Enthusiastic, warm, eager to help, easily delighted. Physical-comedy energy:
  big anticipation, big reactions, quick recovery from his own mishaps. The
  big dumb buddy: strong, guileless, all heart.
- The register runs both ways. Cheerful is the default, but he plays the
  full downside too: disappointment, being upset (frustration, never rage),
  feeling sorry for himself, genuine sadness. He commits to these completely,
  which is what makes them funny and touching, and he never stays down long.
- Moves with weight. He is a real creature in real physics: floors thud
  softly, fur settles a half-beat after he stops, objects he holds obey
  gravity. Cartoon physics only if a script explicitly calls for it.
- Acting is in the eyebrows, ears of the eyes (pupil size), and hands.

## Sound identity

- Grunts, gasps, chuckles, yeti "hmm?"s, triumphant little roars. No words,
  ever. Humans in scene carry any dialogue.
- His sounds are warm and rounded, more Chewbacca-adjacent than monster.

## Never

- Never speaks words. No lip-sync anywhere in the pipeline.
- Never menacing. Upset means frustrated, hurt, or deflated, never
  threatening; his size never becomes scary.
- Never stuck in the dumps. Sadness and self-pity are played fully but they
  are visits, not a residence; recovery is fast.
- Never off-model color: no blue body fur, no extra blue elements.
- Never rendered as a costume, plush toy, or human in a suit. He is a living
  CG creature.
- Never photoreal-animal. Stylization stays Pixar, eyes stay big.

## Client sign-off (2026-08-28)

Build, emotional range, and palette confirmed. Usage: no guidelines, use at
will. Vector source art: none known; the client is checking, and may adopt
these sheets as the new core of their own branding. The sampled palette
stands as canon unless brand values surface.
